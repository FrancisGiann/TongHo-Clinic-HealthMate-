# HealthMate+ user guide

## Administrator

- Sign in with password and the emailed OTP.
- Open **Settings → User management** to invite administrators, nurses, students, or guardians.
- Link student and guardian accounts to an existing patient record before sending the invitation.
- Review audit events, account status, clinic settings, backups, and risk-rule versions.

## Nurse

- Register or update patient profiles.
- Record visits, pain score, clinical observations, treatment, disposition, and follow-up.
- Review the explainable risk result. It is not a diagnosis. High-risk results require a documented acknowledgement and appropriate clinical action.
- Manage appointments, medicine dispensing, and stock.
- Health screening and incident reporting are not available in the current app. Historical records from earlier versions remain in the database but are not shown in the app, reports, or exports.
- Generate daily, weekly, monthly, or custom reports and save snapshots.

## Student and guardian

- Activate the emailed invitation and create a strong password.
- Sign in with password and OTP.
- View only the records linked to the account.
- Request an appointment and monitor its status.
- Contact the clinic directly for urgent or emergency concerns; online scheduling is not an emergency service.

## Backups and privacy

- Export backups only to an approved encrypted location.
- Do not share accounts, OTP codes, exports, or screenshots containing health information.
- Administrators should deactivate unused accounts immediately.
- Report incorrect guardian links or unauthorized access to the school administrator.
