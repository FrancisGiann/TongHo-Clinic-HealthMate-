## Deployment and study decisions needed

1. **Clinical rules:** Obtain signed approval from a licensed PTHI clinician for each risk indicator, weight, threshold, family-alert recipient and text, escalation path, and review/versioning procedure. Test the approved version with fictional cases before enabling alerts.
2. **Paper records:** No authorized historical dataset or actual school paper records were available for this implementation. Obtain PTHI authorization, source data, ownership and access rules, field mapping, duplicate-resolution policy, backup, and a nurse-reviewed staging pilot before any future import. Launch may use newly entered, consented records and fictional staging data; do not claim legacy migration or use actual PTHI records in development screenshots or tests.
3. **Reports:** Approve automated and manual snapshot retention, backup and restore policy, and staff access. Apply database migrations before enabling `REPORT_ARCHIVE_SCHEDULER_ENABLED` in production. Verify each completed period, deduplication, role suppression, and retention on the deployed service.
4. **SMS OTP:** Choose a provider and sender identity; approve message costs, credentials/secrets storage, delivery regions, expiry, rate limits, retry and failure behavior, and an operational test number. Build and test real delivery only after those decisions. No simulated SMS should satisfy the paper claim.
5. **Trusted devices and sessions:** The server currently expires sessions after 15 minutes idle or 12 hours absolute, caps accounts at three active sessions, and supports listing, individual revocation, and password-confirmed sign-out everywhere. “Keep me signed in” only makes the cookie persistent up to that same 12-hour cap; it does not mark a trusted device. Review whether this policy fits shared clinic devices before deployment and keep device binding out of the claim until implemented and verified.
6. **Production checks:** Confirm the Hostinger Business/Cloud and separate Supabase configuration, verified TLS, database migrations and backups, SMTP and OTP delivery, trusted proxy hop count and observed client IP, failed-delivery handling, and guardian/nurse/administrator access with fictional records. Verify in-process scheduler persistence and restart behavior in staging before enabling timers. A successful local test is not proof of production delivery.
7. **Study evaluation:** Define authorized guardian, nurse, and administrator participants, tasks, measures, consent/privacy handling, baseline or comparison, analysis, and limitations. If K–Grade 10 students participate, obtain guardian permission and age-appropriate assent; do not treat students as online account holders. Record actual results before claiming reduced time, fewer errors, satisfaction, or clinical benefit.
8. **Paper visuals and citations:** Review the manuscript's older flowcharts and UI screenshots against the final application, and verify each academic citation against its source before submission. The corrected copy updates inaccurate prose and removes obsolete database and Vanilla JavaScript illustrations; it does not certify every historic figure or citation.
